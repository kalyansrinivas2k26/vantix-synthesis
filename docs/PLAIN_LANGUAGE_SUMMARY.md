
# Plain-Language Summary

VANTIX Synthesis sits above the first four VANTIX projects.

The first four projects answer domain-specific questions:
- Is Salesforce governance healthy?
- Is delivery work ready, supported and approved?
- Was a customer commitment actually fulfilled?
- Are commitment, service-recovery and customer-momentum outcomes properly evidenced?

Synthesis answers a different question:

**What do those signals mean together?**

It does not replace the source projects or average their scores. It looks for six governed relationship types: duplicate, repeated, related, compounding, contradictory and unresolved.

The AI role is intentionally limited. AI can explain a correlation that deterministic logic has already established. It cannot invent the correlation, change source severity, alter source evidence, combine Six Sigma denominators, approve escalation or close a customer outcome.

The current project uses synthetic source fixtures and offline exact-node execution. It is a Portfolio Preview, not a production integration.
